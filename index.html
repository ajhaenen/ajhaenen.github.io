Uptick counts the sets you actually do, not the workout you planned. Five pushups now, ten more later, and the day fills in.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • A target that climbs. Say where you are now and where you want to be. Uptick builds the daily numbers in between, with rest days built in and a step back after each one.
  • Guided sessions. Today's remaining number split into sets, with a rest timer and a beep between them.
  • An interval timer. Work, rest, repeat. Presets for the ones you use often.
  • Routines. A place to keep workouts so you are not standing in a gym trying to remember what you meant to do.
  • Readable. Three display sizes that scale the whole app, not just the text.
Your training data stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no cloud. Every set you log is stored on your device and nowhere else. The app is free and carries one small ad banner, which is what pays for it.
